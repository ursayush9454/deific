Kanpur Nagar Nigam

Kanpur E-Rickshaw Management System

Digital registration, document verification, route management and a police e-challan system for Kanpur’s e-rickshaws, with a centralized dashboard and mobile app.

  • 70,000E-rickshaws registered
  • LiveFully implemented

A Digital Transformation Initiative by Deific Digital

Client
Kanpur Nagar Nigam, Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh
Proposed By
Deific Digital
Project Status
Fully Implemented
Scale
70,000 E-Rickshaws Registered

The Challenge

Kanpur faced challenges in managing a large number of e-rickshaws, including unorganized operations, incomplete documentation, unauthorized route movements and limited monitoring. These issues affected traffic discipline, regulatory compliance and public safety.

The Solution

Deific Digital proposed and executed a comprehensive E-Rickshaw Management Software with Mobile Application, under the guidance of the concerned Divisional Commissioner and Municipal Commissioner, Kanpur, as per the project’s official work order.

The system includes:

  • Digital Registration: Centralized registration and identity records for 70,000 e-rickshaws.
  • Document Verification: Verification and monitoring of vehicle and driver documents.
  • Route Management: Defined and recorded routes for better traffic regulation.
  • Police E-Challan System: Enables authorized traffic police personnel to issue challans for applicable route violations and document-related offences.
  • Centralized Dashboard & Mobile App: Real-time access to records, monitoring, compliance and enforcement workflows.

Impact & Outcomes

  • 70,000 e-rickshaws registered on the platform.
  • Improved visibility of vehicle and driver identities.
  • Better monitoring of documentation and route compliance.
  • Stronger enforcement through digital challan management.
  • Support for improved traffic discipline and passenger safety.
  • Greater transparency and public confidence in e-rickshaw regulation.

The Result

Deific Digital delivered a technology-driven solution to help transform e-rickshaw management in Kanpur into a more organized, traceable and accountable system.

Deific Digital — Smart Technology for Smarter Governance.

Green Park Stadium, Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh

Project Overview

To enhance the digital experience and management of Green Park’s sports and fitness facilities, Deific Digital developed a proposed integrated digital solution combining website development, facility information, online booking capabilities and access control for the gym, tennis courts and badminton courts.

The objective was to make facility access more organized, improve administrative efficiency and provide users with a convenient digital experience.

The Challenge

Managing multiple sports and fitness facilities requires an organized approach to bookings, member access and facility usage. Manual processes can create administrative workload, access verification challenges and limited visibility into facility operations.

The Solution

Deific Digital’s solution brings website-based services and access management together through the following features:

  • Website Development: A centralized digital platform for Green Park facilities, information and services.
  • Online Booking: Convenient access to facility information and booking workflows.
  • Gym Access Control: Controlled entry for authorized gym members.
  • Tennis & Badminton Court Access: Organized access management for sports facilities.
  • Digital Verification: Support for QR/RFID-based entry, subject to deployed hardware and configuration.
  • Administrative Monitoring: Centralized management of access records, memberships and facility usage.

Key Benefits

  • Improved digital accessibility and user convenience.
  • More organized facility booking and entry management.
  • Better control over authorized access.
  • Reduced dependence on manual verification processes.
  • Improved visibility into facility usage and administration.
  • Support for a modern, technology-enabled sports environment.

The Outcome

The Green Park digital solution demonstrates how website technology and access management can work together to support smarter sports-facility operations. By integrating digital services with controlled entry workflows, Deific Digital aims to deliver a more convenient, secure and efficient experience for administrators and facility users.
