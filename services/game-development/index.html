How we work

From first call to live in four steps

Concept and design

We agree the idea, core loop, audience and monetisation plan, and prepare a playable prototype.

Production

We build levels, art, sound and the backend in short sprints, with a build you can play each sprint.

Testing and launch

We test on real devices, fix issues and help you publish on Google Play, the App Store or the web.

Live operations

After launch we track player data, fix bugs and ship updates.

FAQ

Common questions

Which engines and platforms do you use?

We build with engines such as Unity for Android and iOS, and with web technologies for browser games. We pick what fits your game and budget.

Can you build a game from just an idea?

Yes. We help shape the concept, plan the core gameplay and build a prototype before full production.

Do you help publish the game?

Yes. We prepare store listings and builds and support you through publishing on Google Play and the App Store.

How long does a game take to build?

A simple casual game can take a few weeks. Larger games take several months. We give a timeline after the concept discussion.
