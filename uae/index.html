Automation flows that take 60–80% of the routine off your team

A flow connects your WhatsApp, phone, website, ads, CRM, Google Sheets and accounting software, so work moves from one step to the next without anyone pushing it. Here is a typical lead flow we set up.

Lead comes in

WhatsApp, call, website form, Meta or Google ads, property portals

AI agent replies

Within seconds, any time of day, in the customer's language

Lead is qualified

Budget, need and timeline captured; scored hot, warm or cold

Hot lead

Meeting booked on your calendar, sales person alerted on WhatsApp

Warm or cold lead

Follow-up messages run on a schedule until the lead replies

Every step is saved to your CRM or Google Sheet, and the owner gets one summary report at the end of the day.

Questions business owners ask us

What is AI automation for business?

AI automation means software agents and workflows do the repetitive work your team does every day, like replying to leads, answering calls, sending reminders, collecting documents and updating records. Your team only handles what really needs a person.

How much does an AI agent cost in the UAE?

Our AI agents start from AED 899 a month, with a one-time setup from AED 1,999. WhatsApp and telephony charges are billed at actual cost. After the free audit you get a fixed quote in AED.

Do your AI agents speak Arabic?

Yes. Our voice and WhatsApp agents talk with your customers in Arabic and English, and can also handle Hindi and Urdu.

Do you work with businesses across the UAE?

Yes. We work with businesses in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the rest of the UAE. Our delivery team is in India, so we meet on video call and WhatsApp and are available during UAE business hours.

Do you use the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. We set up your number on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, so you get a verified business profile, templates, broadcasts and no risk of a banned number.

Is my business and customer data safe?

Yes. Deific Digital is ISO/IEC 27001 certified for information security and ISO 9001 certified for quality management. Your data is handled under these certified security processes.

How long does it take to go live?

A single agent or flow is usually live in one to two weeks. A full automation setup across sales, support and operations takes a few weeks, depending on the scope agreed in the blueprint.
