Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 12, 2026

You can ask Deific Digital to delete your data from PropAI at any time. Here is how.

1. How to Request Deletion

  • Send an email to hello@deificdigital.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.
  • Include your WhatsApp phone number (with country code) or your registered account email.
  • We may ask you to verify your identity to protect your data.
Email a deletion request

2. What Happens Next

  • We will delete your data within 7 days and confirm by email.
  • Deleted data includes your messages, media, profile information and account data linked to your number or email.
  • Some records, such as billing and tax records, may be kept where required by law.
  • Message data that is not deleted on request is automatically removed after 90 days.

3. Messaged a Business Using PropAI?

If you messaged a real estate business that uses PropAI, you can also ask that business directly to delete your data. For details on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
