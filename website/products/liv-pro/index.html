Frequently asked questions

What is LIVpro?

LIVpro is a live streaming, video chat and matching app built by Deific Digital. Users can join live rooms, send gifts, make video calls and follow hosts. Hosts, agencies and coin sellers earn through built-in payout tools.

Who is LIVpro for?

It suits entrepreneurs who want to launch their own live streaming platform, talent agencies that manage hosts, and startups building entertainment or social apps for India, the UAE and the Middle East.

What can users do in the app?

They can watch public, private, VIP-only and ticketed lives, join party rooms and PK battles, send gifts, chat, post moments, follow hosts and start random 1-on-1 video matches.

Which languages does it support?

The app works in English, Hindi and Arabic, and shows local currency and payment options for countries like India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Indonesia, Philippines, Egypt, the USA and the UK.

Is it safe?

Yes. It has 18+ checks, reporting, device-based self-gifting detection and admin approvals. Users must confirm they are 18+, and hosts go through a selfie and liveness check before going live. The app has report and block options, and it flags suspicious gifting such as self-gifting from the same device. Admin roles and super-admin approvals control sensitive actions like payouts.
