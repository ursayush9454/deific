Frequently asked questions

What is Visitsly?

Visitsly is a visit booking AI system for real estate. It replies to every property inquiry on WhatsApp within seconds, qualifies the buyer, scores the lead, books the site visit with reminders and alerts your sales agent, automatically and 24×7.

How is Visitsly different from a normal real estate CRM?

A normal CRM only stores leads and waits for your team to call. Visitsly actually talks to every lead on WhatsApp, finds out who is serious and books the visit by itself. It is a WhatsApp AI agent, lead scoring tool and real estate CRM in one.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes, and we set it up for you. You connect your number with a simple Facebook login, and Visitsly runs on the official WhatsApp Business API, so there is no ban risk like unofficial bulk tools.

Which lead sources does it work with?

Any source: Facebook and Instagram lead ads, Google Ads, your website forms, portals like Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle, 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com, or a simple Excel upload.

Will the AI give wrong information to buyers?

The AI answers from the projects and properties you add in Visitsly. You can read every conversation, and anything it cannot answer is passed to your agent.

Can I import my old leads?

Yes. Upload an Excel sheet with name, mobile, email, address and job title, then send a bulk WhatsApp or email campaign. The AI takes over every reply.

What about Meta / WhatsApp charges?

Meta charges per conversation. You simply recharge a wallet inside Visitsly, with no need to add your own card to Meta. These charges are separate from your plan.

Do you work in India and the UAE?

Yes. Visitsly is used by real estate teams across India (Delhi NCR, Noida, Lucknow, Kanpur, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru) and the UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah). Pricing is available in INR for India and AED for the UAE.
