Everything inside the app

Chat, voice, video, live rooms, a prepaid wallet and safety tools, all in one place so you can just talk.

Ways to talk

  • Chat with a typing indicator and live timer
  • Voice calls with noise suppression
  • Video calls that blur if no face is in frame
  • Mute and speaker are one tap away
  • Random Chat, Voice or Video with a free listener
  • Live Rooms: host-led voice and video rooms with gifts

Pay per minute

  • Rates shown in coins per minute, 1 coin = ₹1
  • Timer starts when the conversation starts
  • A partial minute counts as a full minute
  • ₹0 if the listener doesn’t accept

Privacy and safety

  • Your number stays hidden
  • Links are blocked in chat
  • Listeners see only your first name
  • Use any name instead of your real one
  • Report any session in one tap

Listeners and partners

  • ID and profile verification for every partner

  • Earn for every minute you help someone
  • Withdraw earnings from the Partner app
  • Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees

Wallet

  • Prepaid wallet, no surprise bills
  • UPI, card or netbanking via Razorpay
  • Packs add bonus coins
  • Low-balance warning, then the session ends

What Bondty is not

  • Not a doctor, therapist or emergency service
  • Not dating, not therapy
  • In an emergency, call local emergency services

The problem

Sometimes you just need someone to talk to, but friends and family aren’t always available or the right people to share everything with. Many people carry stress, loneliness or worries alone because they fear being judged.

Who needs it

  • Students dealing with exam stress
  • Working professionals under pressure
  • People feeling lonely or low late at night
  • Anyone who wants to vent without judgement
  • Adults 18+ who want a private conversation

Frequently asked questions

How am I charged?

Each listener’s rate is shown in coins per minute, and 1 coin = ₹1. The timer starts when the conversation starts. A partial minute counts as a full minute.

What if the listener doesn’t accept my request?

You aren’t charged. Nothing leaves your wallet until the conversation starts.

What if my balance runs out mid-session?

The session ends automatically. You get a warning when your balance is low, and you can top up to keep talking.

Can the listener see my number?

No. Listeners only see your first name, and you can use any name instead of your real one.

Is Bondty free to download?

Yes. Downloading and browsing are free. You pay only for conversation time from a prepaid wallet.

Is Bondty therapy or an emergency service?

No. Bondty is a conversation platform only. It is not a doctor, therapist or emergency service, and it is not dating. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Can I become a listener?

Yes. Partners go through ID and profile verification, go online to accept chat, voice or video requests, and earn for every minute. Earnings are withdrawn from the Partner app.
