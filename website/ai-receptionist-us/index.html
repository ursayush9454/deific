Frequently asked questions

What does the AI receptionist do?

It answers your clinic's phone 24/7, books and reschedules appointments in your calendar, saves every inquiry as a lead, calls no-shows to rebook, calls patients for feedback, and sends you a daily report.

Does it sound like a robot?

No — it speaks naturally and handles interruptions. Call our live demo at +1 (771) 222-1044 and try booking a cleaning.

Can I keep my clinic's phone number?

Yes. Your current number forwards calls to the AI — for example only when the front desk doesn't pick up, when the line is busy, or after hours.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

It's built for HIPAA: encrypted patient data, two-factor login, audit logs and automatic logoff. We sign a Business Associate Agreement with your clinic and use vendors that sign BAAs with us.

How much does it cost?

A simple monthly fee based on your clinic's size and call volume — far less than a $26/hour receptionist. Book a demo for a quote.
